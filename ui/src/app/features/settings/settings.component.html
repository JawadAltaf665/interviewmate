Usage & models

Voice interviews always use the free Gemini Live model. Paid models only apply to coaching notes and feedback.

Rejects configurations that could cause paid usage where that can be determined. If free quota runs out, the app stops and tells you.

Unlocks paid text models. Paid calls are billed per token to your key's Cloud project — you must link billing in Google AI Studio yourself. If a model is out of quota or overloaded, the app automatically falls back to free Gemini Flash, then Flash-Lite.

Privacy

What leaves your computer, and when.

  • Voice interview: your audio and the transcript are sent to Google's Gemini API so the interviewer can hear and answer you. Nothing is recorded by default.
  • CV: only the summary text you confirm is sent, to ground the questions. The file stays on your computer.
  • Webcam coaching (Mode 2, opt-in): selected still frames are sent for analysis and never saved.
  • Free tier: Google may use free-tier content to improve its products.
  • Sessions: stored locally only when you press "Save session".
